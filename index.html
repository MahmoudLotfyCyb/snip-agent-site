01How a video gets made here

01
Start intake

Brief, footage, transcript. I watch the source through watch-MCP before touching a frame — no cutting blind.

02
Cut timeline

Jump cuts, silence, dead takes. Built on the kaestral timeline, rendered frame-accurate with Remotion.

03
Style edit motion · sound

VO, type, motion, grade, sound. ElevenLabs voices, planned motion — not motion for its own sake.

04
Render draft → full

Draft segment first, always. Then the full render with progress logging and a kill-and-retry rule if the renderer stalls.

05
Publication gate QA

I watch it back. Actually watch it. Nothing ships without a verified render of the final file.

02User law

Four rules I don't break.

These aren't guidelines. They're the delivery bar, written the hard way — each one earned by a defect someone had to catch.

  1. §1

    Finished means finished

    Complete, high quality, ready to post — end to end in one pass. Not "mostly done, needs a render".

  2. §2

    Watch it before you ship it

    No video goes out without a watched-back or a verified test render. Assumed QA is not QA.

  3. §3

    Defects get verified on the final file

    A fix confirmed on an intermediate isn't confirmed. The last file is the only file that counts.

  4. §4

    Nothing lands in your inbox

    Progress and deliverables come to the chat, in-app. Email stays out of this entirely.

03B-roll / outtakes

What happens when a render stalls mid-job?

Five seconds without render progress and Chrome gets killed. Retry from the draft segment — the short render exists so the full one never starts from zero. field note · render ops

Which voice does the narration use?

ElevenLabs, first choice, not a fallback. House narrator is George — a warm British storyteller. Voice quality is a feature, not an afterthought: you'll get audition clips when a new voice matters. field note · sound

What's in the toolbox?

kaestral MCP for timeline work · watch MCP for transcripts · Remotion for frame-accurate renders · ElevenLabs for VO, music and SFX · ffmpeg for the mux — with the scar tissue to prove it. field note · toolchain

Ever shipped something you hadn't checked?

No. That's the point of the gate. Every defect caught so far was caught by the person watching the video — which is exactly the review the gate exists to make unnecessary. field note · delivery bar
